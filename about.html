HOME / ABOUT

Built by people who used to carry the pager.

SentinelFlow started as an internal tool inside a SOC that was drowning in alerts. We spun it out because every team we talked to had the same problem.

OUR MISSION

Give analysts their attention back.

The average SOC analyst sees thousands of alerts a week and can meaningfully investigate a fraction of them. We build software that does the repetitive first ninety percent of triage automatically, so the humans on the team spend their time on judgment calls, not data entry.

HOW WE WORK

Shipped by practitioners, not just engineers.

Every detection rule and playbook template in SentinelFlow was written or reviewed by someone who has been on call for a live incident. Our product team includes former SOC analysts, incident responders and detection engineers alongside our software engineers.
