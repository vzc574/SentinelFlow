1. Overview

SentinelFlow SOAR ("SentinelFlow", "we", "us") provides security orchestration, automation and response software to security operations teams. This policy explains what information we collect through our website and product, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

2. Data we collect

We collect a few categories of information depending on how you interact with us:

  • Account data — name, work email, company, and role when you register or start a trial.
  • Product telemetry — security events, alerts and case metadata that you connect into the platform, processed on your behalf to deliver the service.
  • Usage data — pages visited, features used and general device information, collected to improve the product.
  • Support communications — anything you send us through the contact form, email or live chat.

3. How we use data

We use collected data to operate and secure the platform, respond to support requests, send product and billing communications, and improve detection and automation quality. We do not use customer case data to train models that are shared across other customers.

4. Sharing & disclosure

We share data only with subprocessors that help us run the service — such as cloud infrastructure and email delivery providers — under contracts that require them to protect it. We disclose data when required by law, or to investigate fraud or abuse. We never sell personal data.

5. Security measures

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Access to production systems is limited to staff who need it, protected by single sign-on and multi-factor authentication, and logged for audit. Our controls are assessed annually as part of our SOC 2 Type II audit.

6. Data retention

Account and billing data is retained for the life of your subscription and for a limited period afterward to meet legal and accounting obligations. Case and log data you send into the platform is retained according to the retention window configured on your account, and is deleted or exportable on request after cancellation.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete personal data we hold about you, and to object to certain processing. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below and we'll respond within 30 days.

8. Cookies

Our website uses a small number of cookies and local storage entries: one to remember your light/dark theme preference, and session cookies required to keep you signed in. We do not use third-party advertising cookies.

9. Contact us

Questions about this policy can be sent to privacy@sentinelflow.io, or through the contact page.
